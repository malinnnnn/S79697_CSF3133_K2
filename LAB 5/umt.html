About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science at Mengabang Telipot, providing essential facilities for students and lecturers to conduct practical sessions and marine research.

In June 1996, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science was transferred from Serdang to Kuala Terengganu, transforming the centre into a branch campus named Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, it gained autonomy as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) before officially adopting the name Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across multiple specialized faculties, focusing on marine science, computing, engineering, business, and sustainable development.
